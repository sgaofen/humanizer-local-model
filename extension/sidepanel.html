Your words, a little more human.

Select a passage in your Doc and click Humanize selection, or use the right-click option.

Local app connection

Start the Humanizer app first. Click Connect on the page that opens. Reconnect after restarting the app.

If Docs can’t share the selection, copy it with Ctrl+C / ⌘C, then click Use copied text.

Open a Google Doc to get started.

Review the result, then copy it and paste over the selected passage in Docs. This replaces it as plain text; use Undo in Docs to revert.
