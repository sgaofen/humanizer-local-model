PRODUCT RECALL STATUS UPDATE — Model X-200 Thermostat
Prepared by: S. Park, Product Safety & Compliance
Date: September 21, 2026

Recall Summary: The Model X-200 smart thermostat recall (initiated August 4, 2026, per CPSC filing #26-417) remains active due to a wiring defect in the R-terminal connector that can cause overheating under sustained HVAC load. Of the 5,000 units shipped between January 15 and June 30, 2026, 1,800 units (36%) have been recovered as of today.

Recovery Breakdown:
- Retail returns (Home Depot, Lowe's, and authorized dealers): 1,120 units
- Direct mail-back kits processed by our Reno, NV service center: 480 units
- Field technician swaps for units installed via professional installers: 200 units

Outstanding: 3,200 units remain unaccounted for. Regional distribution suggests the largest concentration of unrecovered units is in the Southeast (est. 1,100 units, largely tied to a bulk order fulfilled through Coastal Home Builders in March), followed by the Midwest (est. 900 units).

Actions in Progress:
- A second-wave email and SMS notification campaign launches September 25, targeting the 3,200 registered owners who have not responded to the first two outreach attempts (August 6 and August 20).
- Coastal Home Builders has agreed to provide site access logs for the 340 homes in its March order by September 30 to enable direct technician outreach.
- Customer support (ext. 4420) is fielding an average of 45 recall-related calls per day; average case resolution time is 6.2 days.

Next Milestone: Target 3,000 units recovered by October 31, 2026. Weekly recovery rate has averaged 210 units over the past four weeks; at current pace we project reaching that target by November 3, roughly three days behind schedule.

Please direct questions to the Product Safety team before the October 1 leadership review.
